Overview

Framing is the core of what Matrix Homes does. Basements, additions, garages and new homes: if it needs a frame, our crew builds it straight, square and ready for every trade that follows.

A good frame makes every later trade faster and every finish better. Walls are plumb, floors are flat, openings are exactly where the drawings put them, and the trusses and sheathing go on clean.

Homeowners hire us for complete framing packages on custom homes and additions. Builders and developers bring in our framing crew for new homes and multi-unit work.

What this covers

  • New home framing
  • Addition framing
  • Basement framing
  • Garage framing
  • Roof truss installation
  • Wall and roof sheathing
  • Vaulted ceilings, lofts and stair openings
  • Framing crew for builders and developers

Planning considerations

Before work begins

Drawings and layout

We review the framing plans and truss layout before the crew arrives, so questions are answered before lumber is cut.

Lumber and trusses

Material and truss deliveries are timed to the schedule so the site stays organised and safe.

Inspections

Framing inspections are part of the permit process. We plan the sequence so the frame is ready on time.

Trades that follow

Plumbing, HVAC and electrical rough-ins depend on the frame. Openings and blocking are planned with them in mind.

Questions about framing

Is your framing crew available for builders?

Yes. Matrix Homes regularly frames for builders and developers. Send the plans and timing through the quote form or call (416) 294-7713.

Do you install trusses and sheathing?

Yes. Truss installation and wall and roof sheathing are part of our framing work.

Do you frame basements?

Yes. Basement framing is one of our most common projects, on its own or as part of a full basement renovation.
